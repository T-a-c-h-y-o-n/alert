Frequently asked questions

What is an FDA Import Alert?

Guidance to FDA field personnel identifying firms or products subject to detention without physical examination (DWPE) or other enforcement attention. The source of truth is always the official FDA record.

What do Red, Yellow, and Green mean?

Red List: detention without physical examination under the applicable alert. Yellow List: intensified surveillance. Green List: exempt from DWPE under that alert - never a universal declaration of safety or compliance. Not every alert uses all three lists.

What does the report contain?

Up to 25 verified firm records with alert numbers, exact list types, product codes, official source links, verification dates, plus the source-level audit log, methodology, coverage limits, and disclaimer.

How are the source records checked?

Each row is observed directly on its official FDA Import Alert page. The date checked is kept separate from the FDA page's own publication date, and every record is traceable to the audit log.

What does the report not establish?

It does not determine legal violations, detention outcomes, FSVP compliance, or whether 21 CFR 1.505 applies to any specific transaction. Advisory only - verify on the official FDA page before acting.

How can I verify the official record myself?

Click the FDA source link in any row and compare firm spelling, address, product code, and alert number. Sources: IA 16-119 and IA 16-124 on accessdata.fda.gov.
